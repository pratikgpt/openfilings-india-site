OpenFilings India

A planned free, non-commercial archive of the disclosures that Indian listed companies file with the stock exchanges, starting with quarterly financial results.

Status (November 2026): research stage. No data is published, and no automated collection is running. Public access will only start with the exchanges' written permission or legal advice.

What it is for

  • Turning companies' XBRL results filings into consistent, comparable financial statements.
  • Keeping a full version history, so it is clear when a company revised a number it had already published.
  • Linking every number back to the official filing it came from.
  • Serving students, researchers, journalists, small investors and independent developers.

What it will not do

  • No price or market data.
  • No private companies.
  • No investment advice, ratings or signals.
  • No advertising and no paid tiers.
  • No re-hosting of exchange documents without permission.

For website operators: our bot

If you see requests from this project, they identify themselves with this User-Agent:

OpenFilingsIndia/<version> (+https://pratikgpt.github.io/openfilings-india-site/; <project email>)

The software enforces these rules in code:

  • Only sources whose terms allow it, or whose owners have given permission, are contacted.
  • At most one request every 5 seconds per host, over one connection.
  • File downloads only between 01:00 and 06:00 IST. Feeds are read at most every 15 minutes.
  • robots.txt is checked before every run, and conditional requests avoid downloading unchanged files.
  • It backs off after an error or a "slow down" response, and stops completely after a few in a row.
  • It never disguises itself as a browser, reuses cookies, rotates IP addresses or uses proxies.

If you want it to slow down or stop, tell us (below) and it will be done, no questions asked.
